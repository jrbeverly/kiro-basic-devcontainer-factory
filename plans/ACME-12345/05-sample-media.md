---
title: Add a known-valid MP4 to test with
---

Add a short MP4 that is known-good input for transcription, either committed or
fetched by a helper. Speech is worth more than silence, but a file that already
exists and is known to work is worth more than a way to generate one.

It sits where the end-to-end check can reach it, in `examples/default`.

Files: `examples/default/`, and `scripts/` if it is fetched rather than committed.
